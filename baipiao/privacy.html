被白嫖了么 · 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 2 日

一句话：这个 App 不收集你的任何数据。没有账号，没有服务器，没有广告，没有第三方统计。你在 App 里输入和产生的所有信息都只保存在你自己的手机里。

我们存了什么、存在哪里

你填写的工资和工时设置、加班记录、公司位置、打卡照片，全部保存在 App 在你设备上的沙盒目录中（锁屏小组件需要的少量数据保存在同一设备的 App Group 共享区）。这些数据不会上传到我们或任何第三方的服务器。删除 App 即删除全部数据。

位置

如果你开启"自动记录"，App 会向 iOS 注册一个以你设定的公司位置为中心的地理围栏，由系统在你进入或离开该区域时通知 App。App 只记录"进入 / 离开"的时刻，不记录你的移动轨迹，位置信息不会离开设备。你可以随时在 iOS 设置中关闭位置权限，App 的其余功能不受影响。

相机与相册

"打卡取证"功能可选用相机拍照。照片加上时间地点水印后保存在 App 沙盒内，不会写入系统相册，也不会上传。

Siri 与快捷指令

App 提供"开始加班 / 结束加班"的快捷指令。相关处理完全在设备上进行；Siri 的语音识别由 Apple 按其隐私政策处理。

分享

你生成的分享图通过 iOS 系统分享面板发送到你自己选择的 App 或保存到相册，发送行为完全由你决定。分享图不包含公司位置和打卡照片。

我们不做的事

不收集设备标识符，不使用广告标识符（IDFA），不接入任何分析或广告 SDK，不出售或共享任何数据。

儿童

本 App 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集任何儿童的信息（事实上不收集任何人的信息）。

变更

如本政策有变更，我们会在此页面更新并修改生效日期。

Boss Owes Me · Privacy Policy

Effective: 2 October 2026

In one sentence: this app does not collect any of your data. There is no account, no server, no advertising and no third-party analytics. Everything you enter or create in the app stays on your own device.

What is stored, and where

Your salary and working-hour settings, overtime records, office location and check-in photos are stored only in the app's sandbox on your device (a small amount of data needed by the Lock Screen widget is stored in the App Group container on the same device). Nothing is uploaded to us or to any third party. Deleting the app deletes all data.

Location

If you turn on automatic tracking, the app registers a single geofence around the office location you set; iOS notifies the app when you enter or leave that area. The app records only those enter/leave moments — never a movement history — and location never leaves the device. You can revoke location permission in iOS Settings at any time; all other features keep working.

Camera and photos

The optional "Proof" check-in can take a photo. The photo is watermarked with time and place and stored inside the app sandbox; it is not written to your photo library and never uploaded.

Siri and Shortcuts

The app provides "Start overtime" / "Stop overtime" shortcuts. All processing happens on device; Siri's speech recognition is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Sharing

Share images are sent through the iOS share sheet to apps you choose, or saved to your photos, entirely at your decision. Share images never include your office location or check-in photos.

What we don't do

We do not collect device identifiers, do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), do not integrate any analytics or advertising SDK, and do not sell or share any data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children (it collects no information from anyone).

Changes

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
